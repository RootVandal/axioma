FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the project: how many pages, whether there is a catalog, whether you want animation. Tell us what you need and we will give you an exact price before we start.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. We tell you the timeline in advance, together with the price.

What do you need from me?

Tell us about your business and send texts and photos if you have them. If something is missing, we will suggest what to do.

Do you only work in Almaty?

No. We are based in Almaty and work remotely with clients from Kazakhstan, the CIS and other countries.

Will the site work on phones?

Yes. We check every site on phones and computers.
